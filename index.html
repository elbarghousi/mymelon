🔗 What is Blockchain?

Blockchain is a revolutionary digital technology that stores information securely across thousands of computers worldwide. Unlike traditional banking systems controlled by one company or government, blockchain is decentralized, transparent and secure. Every transaction is recorded permanently and cannot easily be changed or deleted. Cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum use blockchain technology to allow people to send money directly without banks.

🪙 What is a Meme Coin?

A meme coin is a cryptocurrency inspired by internet culture, memes and online communities. Unlike traditional crypto projects focused mainly on technology, meme coins focus heavily on community, social media and fun branding. Some meme coins become extremely popular thanks to strong online communities, viral marketing and social engagement. Dogecoin and Shiba Inu are famous examples of meme coins that became global crypto phenomena.

🦊 What is MetaMask?

MetaMask is one of the most popular cryptocurrency wallets in the world. It allows users to store, send, receive and swap cryptocurrencies securely. MetaMask also connects users directly to decentralized applications such as Uniswap, NFT marketplaces and blockchain games. The wallet works as both a browser extension and a mobile application, giving users full control over their crypto assets.

💧 What is Liquidity?

Liquidity represents the amount of money and tokens available inside a trading pool. Higher liquidity allows people to buy and sell tokens more easily with smaller price changes. Low liquidity can create strong volatility and large price swings. On decentralized exchanges like Uniswap, liquidity is provided by users called liquidity providers who deposit tokens into pools and earn trading fees in return.
